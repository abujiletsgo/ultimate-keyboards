Language: English (United States; also fallback for `en-CA`). **Draft only — not submitted** (`intent.md`: App Store Connect changes are forbidden until the next submitted version; companion mobile keyboard app `world.hyly.keyboards` is at spec stage in `docs/plan/type-on-android.md` and `docs/plan/type-on-iphone.md`).

Counts are Unicode characters (what Google Play Console and App Store Connect count), except Apple App Store `Keywords`, which are UTF-8 bytes.

**Distribution architecture (`marketing/facts.md`):**
- **Google Play & Apple App Store (iPhone/iPad):** Free wireless keyboard companion app (`world.hyly.keyboards`) that lets you type on your Android phone/tablet or iPhone/iPad from your MacBook keyboard while keeping your Mac's home-row mods, layers and combos.
- **macOS desktop app (`com.ultimatekeyboards.app`):** Ships as an Apple-notarized Developer ID installer at `https://hyly.world/keyboards/` (Apple silicon M1 or newer, macOS 13+), not on the Mac App Store, because the Mac App Store's mandatory App Sandbox blocks global low-level HID keyboard remapping.
